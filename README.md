# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides an overview of workforce size, employee job satisfaction, and employee attrition. It also compares attrition across departments to support human resource analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Employees:** Shows the employee count represented in the dashboard.
- **Average Job Satisfaction:** Summarises the average job satisfaction score.
- **Attrition Count:** Measures the number of employees who have left the organisation, as defined by the underlying dataset.

## Key Insights
1. **Workforce Overview:** The total employee KPI provides a high-level view of the workforce represented in the dataset.

2. **Employee Job Satisfaction:** The average job satisfaction KPI summarises employee satisfaction. It can be used as a starting point for investigating workforce experience and engagement.

3. **Employee Attrition:** The attrition count highlights the number of employee departures recorded in the data and provides a baseline for workforce retention analysis.

4. **Department-Wise Attrition:** The column chart compares attrition counts across departments, helping identify departments that may require closer examination.

5. **Retention Analysis:** Combining the overall attrition count with department-level comparisons helps HR teams investigate potential retention challenges and prioritise further analysis.

## Business Recommendations
- Examine departments with comparatively high attrition counts.
- Investigate potential factors associated with employee departures, such as job satisfaction, workload, career progression, and management practices, if these variables are available.
- Track job satisfaction alongside attrition over time to understand whether changes in employee experience coincide with changes in departures.
- Consider employee headcount by department when comparing attrition counts, because larger departments may naturally have more departures.

## Conclusion
The HR dashboard provides a concise view of employee numbers, average job satisfaction, and attrition. Its department-level comparison helps HR professionals identify areas for further investigation and supports workforce planning and retention decisions.
